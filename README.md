# Niranjan Tapasvi

I build AI systems that are meant to be trusted, not just capable — with a focus on agent safety, perception, and applied machine learning.

MS Data Science, Indiana University Bloomington. Previously at the Vehicle Autonomy and Intelligence Lab, Sendblue, and Opine Group.

## Background

Every role I've had comes back to the same problem: systems fail quietly when the data or decisions behind them can't be trusted, and by the time someone notices, it's already expensive to fix.

At Opine Group and Sendblue, that meant catching bad data before it reached a dashboard. At the Vehicle Autonomy and Intelligence Lab, it meant finding exactly where perception models break — glare, occlusion, motion blur — before they break in the real world.

I build the same kind of tools outside of work: systems that catch failure before it happens rather than after.

## Projects

### Z3 AI Agent Safety Checker
A pre-execution safety layer for AI agents. Every planned tool call is checked against permissions, budget limits, and forbidden action sequences before the agent is allowed to act.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NiranjanTapasv1/NiranjanTapasv1/main/pipeline-diagram.svg" alt="agent safety pipeline" width="100%" />

Repo: https://github.com/NiranjanTapasv1/z3-agent-safety-checker
Demo: https://www.loom.com/share/9159ac806f3344f6ae013562fee9959f

### Frame2Mesh — 3D Reconstruction from Smartphone Video
An end-to-end pipeline that turns smartphone video into a dense point cloud and clean 3D mesh, using COLMAP and Screened Poisson reconstruction. Built on multi-view geometry, no generative fill-in.
Repo: https://github.com/NiranjanTapasv1/frame2mesh-3d-reconstruction

### GEO Drift Tracker
Tracks how AI answers to the same question shift across Gemini and Groq, so a brand can see when it's being described inconsistently across models.
Repo: https://github.com/NiranjanTapasv1/AI-Visiblity-Drift · Live: https://ai-visiblity-drift-8t9ywwcijebgn3pohxfhhx.streamlit.app/

### Mem0 GrowthOS
A growth intelligence platform that finds developers hitting the AI memory wall in real time and helps a GTM team reach them before they build something custom instead.
Repo: https://github.com/NiranjanTapasv1/Mem0-growthOS · Live: https://mem0-growthos.onrender.com/
